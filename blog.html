WE ACTUALLY DID IT!

500 seltzers reviewed, a brand new website, and a huge week for everything defnot_matty. Here's what's going on.

By Matt · Apr 29, 2026 · 5 min read See Full Rankings →

This has been a HUGE week for spiked seltzers, streaming, content, and me personally. We finally launched The Seltzer Review, hit the big 500 Spiked Seltzer reviews, cleaned up the YouTube page and started posting more content consistently, and we're still gaming and yapping all night long over at Twitch! I can't be prouder to share with everyone all of the cool stuff I've been working on.

Just being able to launch an actual website is a tremendous feat for someone like me. I'm basically a caveman when it comes to tech stuff like this, and when I started, I didn't have the foggiest idea of how to build this. It really is a testament to anyone that if you think of something that brings you excitement, and you back that with focus and passion, you can do anything you set your mind to!

"I'm drinking it, so you don't have to." — Matt

So what's the point of this blog anyway? Well, frankly I just wanted a place where I could store my stream of consciousness on this website. I wanted my own corner of the internet where I can talk about what I'm doing, what's going on in my life, what is intriguing to me in this current moment, and any other random crap I can think of. Most of this stuff I'll be talking about live on stream, so if you find yourself over there, definitely talk to me about it! Maybe this is something you'll be interested in too? If so, welcome aboard!
